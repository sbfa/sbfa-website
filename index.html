Who We Are

The Small Business Fleet Alliance (SBFA) unites independent drivers and gig workers—box trucks, hotshot pickups, Sprinters, cargo vans, delivery drivers, and small service operators—into one purchasing group.

You run your own business, but you shouldn’t have to pay full retail while big fleets get the discounts.

We’re building group buying power so independent operators and gig workers can access better rates on fuel, parts, service, and more.

Independent operators and gig workers get real buying power — without giving up ownership of their business.
