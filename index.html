Who we are

You run your own business. Buy like a fleet.

The Small Business Fleet Alliance (SBFA) brings independent operators together so they can access better rates than they can get alone.

You run your own business — with a truck, a trailer, or your own tools. You shouldn’t have to pay full retail while large fleets get preferred pricing on fuel, lodging, tools, and more.
