Become a Service Partner

Get in front of independent commercial drivers in your area. Partner with the Small Business Fleet Alliance and grow with the local driver community.

Who This Is For

Local shops and service providers that help independent drivers keep their trucks on the road.

Mechanics & Repair Shops

Independent and local shops that service box trucks, hotshots, sprinters, and commercial vans.

Tire Shops

Shops that sell and service commercial tires and want steady independent driver business.

Mobile Repair & Roadside

Mobile mechanics and roadside services that can support drivers where they are.

Other Service Providers

Truck washes, detailing, upfitting, towing, and other businesses that serve commercial operators.

What You Get

Exposure to Local Drivers

Reach active independent commercial drivers in your city through the Alliance network.

Preferred Partner Status

Get listed as a preferred service partner for SBFA members in your area.

Referrals from Chapter Leaders

Chapter Leaders and members can send business your way when drivers need service.

Ability to Offer Member Perks

Offer exclusive member perks (e.g., 10% off labor, free alignment with purchase of 4 commercial tires, or priority same-day service for Alliance drivers).

What We Look For

Reliable Service

Shops that treat independent drivers fairly and get them back on the road quickly.

Fair Pricing

Transparent, competitive rates that make sense for owner-operators.

Willingness to Support Members

Interest in offering a simple member perk or priority service for Alliance drivers.

Local Presence

Businesses rooted in the communities where our members work and live.
