How it saves money: Solo drivers and single-member LLCs can shelter up to $72,000/year directly from federal and state taxable income by contributing as both the "employer" and "employee."

TAX REDUCTION STRATEGY

2. S-Corporation Election (Slashing 15.3% Self-Employment Tax)

How it saves money: Once an LLC nets over $60,000–$75,000/year, electing S-Corp status legally splits net income between a W-2 salary and shareholder distributions, shielding distributions from the 15.3% Self-Employment Tax (Social Security & Medicare).

  • ✓ Instant Cash Savings: A business netting $85,000 with a $50,000 reasonable salary saves approximately $5,355 every year in self-employment taxes.
  • ✓ Official IRS Status: Keeps your existing LLC legal structure intact while switching purely how the IRS taxes your business profits.
  • ✓ Automated Compliance: Run your required W-2 officer payroll directly through our OnPay integration to handle tax withholdings automatically.

Direct Setup Steps:

  1. Review annual profit with your tax preparer to confirm your net revenue exceeds $60k.
  2. Download and complete IRS Form 2553 (Election by a Small Business Corporation).
  3. Sign and mail/fax the form to your designated IRS center within 75 days of the start of the tax year (or request late election relief under Rev. Proc. 2013-30).

TRIPLE TAX-ADVANTAGED ACCOUNT

3. Health Savings Account (The Medical Wealth Shield)

How it saves money: An HSA is the single most tax-advantaged account in the US tax code. It offers a triple tax shield: contributions are 100% tax-deductible, investments grow completely tax-free, and withdrawals for medical care are never taxed.

  • ✓ Annual Tax Deductions: Contribute up to $4,300 (Individual) or $8,550 (Family) pre-tax each year.
  • ✓ Never Expires: Unlike an FSA, HSA funds roll over forever and can be invested directly into index funds and stocks.
  • ✓ Secret Retirement Account: After age 65, funds can be withdrawn for regular living expenses penalty-free (taxed as standard income, identical to a 401k).

Direct Setup Steps:

  1. Ensure you are enrolled in an eligible High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP).
  2. Open a $0 monthly fee self-directed investment HSA through Fidelity or Lively.
  3. Automate monthly transfers from your business checking to build an invested medical reserve.

PASSIVE CASH FLOW STRATEGY

4. High-Yield Tax & Maintenance Sinking Funds

How it saves money: Stop letting $15,000–$30,000 in quarterly tax reserves, insurance down payments, and emergency tire funds sit in commercial checking earning 0.01%.

  • ✓ 4%–5% Yield on Operating Cash: Holding $25,000 in dedicated high-yield reserves earns $1,000 to $1,250 a year in passive cash.
  • ✓ State Tax-Exempt T-Bills: Short-term US Treasury Bills (4-week to 13-week) are 100% exempt from state and local income taxes.
  • ✓ Automated Discipline: Separate operating capital from tax money to avoid cash flow crunches when quarterly IRS estimates come due.

Direct Setup Steps:

  1. Open an account on TreasuryDirect or open a dedicated high-yield business savings account.
  2. Set up a rule to sweep 20%–25% of every route settlement into your reserve fund.
  3. Schedule automatic reinvestment for short-term Treasury Bills so reserves continuously roll over.
