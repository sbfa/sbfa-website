1. Self-Employed Solo 401(k) & SEP IRA Suite

Solo drivers and single-member LLCs can shelter up to $72,000/year directly from federal and state taxable income by contributing as both the "employer" and "employee."

  • ✓ Maximum Income Write-Off: Contribute up to $23,500 as employee salary deferral plus up to 25% of net business compensation.
  • ✓ Solo 401(k) Loan Provision: Borrow up to 50% of your balance (up to $50,000) penalty-free for emergency truck repairs or equipment, paying interest back to yourself.
  • ✓ Zero Ongoing Overhead: $0 account opening and maintenance fees through major institutional custodians like Fidelity.

Direct Setup Steps:

  1. Have your Business EIN and business checking account routing number ready.
  2. Select "Individual / Solo 401(k)" (for max write-offs + loan option) or "SEP IRA" (for simpler zero-paperwork profit sharing).
  3. Complete the online application and e-sign the standard IRS Adoption Agreement naming your business as the plan sponsor.

TAX REDUCTION STRATEGY

2. S-Corporation Election (Slashing 15.3% Self-Employment Tax)

Once an LLC nets over roughly $60,000–$75,000/year, electing S-Corp status can legally split net income between a W-2 salary and shareholder distributions, shielding distributions from the 15.3% Self-Employment Tax.

  • ✓ Instant Cash Savings: A business netting $85,000 with a $50,000 reasonable salary can save approximately $5,000+ every year in self-employment taxes.
  • ✓ Keeps Your LLC: The legal structure stays the same; only the tax treatment changes.
  • ✓ Payroll Required: You must run reasonable officer payroll (OnPay or similar makes this easy).

Direct Setup Steps:

  1. Review annual profit with your tax preparer to confirm the strategy makes sense.
  2. Complete and file IRS Form 2553 (Election by a Small Business Corporation).
  3. Set up officer payroll and begin running a reasonable salary.
TRIPLE TAX-ADVANTAGED ACCOUNT

3. Health Savings Account (The Medical Wealth Shield)

An HSA offers a triple tax shield: contributions are 100% tax-deductible, investments grow tax-free, and withdrawals for medical care are never taxed.

  • ✓ Annual Tax Deductions: Contribute up to the current IRS limits for individual or family coverage.
  • ✓ Never Expires: Unlike an FSA, HSA funds roll over forever and can be invested.
  • ✓ Retirement Use: After age 65, funds can be withdrawn for any purpose (taxed as ordinary income, similar to a traditional 401k).

Direct Setup Steps:

  1. Enroll in an eligible High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP).
  2. Open a low- or no-fee self-directed investment HSA (Fidelity or Lively are popular options).
  3. Automate monthly transfers from your business checking.

PASSIVE CASH FLOW STRATEGY

4. High-Yield Tax & Maintenance Sinking Funds

Stop letting quarterly tax reserves, insurance down payments, and emergency tire funds sit in commercial checking earning almost nothing.

  • ✓ 4%–5% Yield on Operating Cash: Holding reserves in high-yield accounts or short-term Treasuries generates meaningful passive income.
  • ✓ State Tax-Exempt T-Bills: Short-term US Treasury Bills are exempt from state and local income taxes.
  • ✓ Automated Discipline: Separate operating capital from tax money to avoid cash-flow crunches.

Direct Setup Steps:

  1. Open an account on TreasuryDirect or a high-yield business savings account.
  2. Sweep 20%–25% of every route settlement into the reserve fund.
  3. Reinvest short-term Treasury Bills so reserves continuously roll over.

ONPAY PAYROLL INTEGRATION

5. Turnkey Company 401(k) (For Fleets With Drivers on Payroll)

Attract and retain drivers with an automated 401(k) that links to payroll. Eligible small businesses can also claim federal startup tax credits under the SECURE 2.0 Act.

  • ✓ Automated Sync: Deductions and employer matches process automatically every payroll cycle.
  • ✓ Fiduciary Support: Integrated providers can act as plan administrators and investment managers.

Direct Setup Steps:

  1. Log into your OnPay (or similar) employer dashboard.
  2. Navigate to the App Directory / Benefits section and select a 401(k) partner.
  3. Activate automated payroll contribution deductions.
