SBFA SMALL BUSINESS FLEET ALLIANCE

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

The Small Business Fleet Alliance ("SBFA," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect through sbfaohio.com and smallbusinessfleetalliance.com (the "Site"), how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It applies together with our Terms of Service.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us. When you join, apply as a chapter leader or partner, submit a referral, or contact us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, business name and type, the kind of vehicle or work you do, and any other details you choose to include in a form or message.
  • Account and payment information. Membership accounts are handled through our membership platform (Memberstack), and payments are handled by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not receive or store your full payment card number. We may receive limited payment details, such as the card brand and last four digits, payment status, and billing email.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting and service providers may automatically collect technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and referring pages.
  • Cookies and tracking technologies. The Site and some of our partner links use cookies, pixels, or similar technologies (see Section 5).

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage memberships, process dues and renewals, and provide member benefits.
  • To respond to questions, applications, referrals, and partner inquiries.
  • To send service messages such as receipts, renewal reminders, and updates about changes to your membership or our terms.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site and our programs.
  • To administer the chapter leader and referral program, including tracking referrals and calculating rewards.
  • To comply with legal obligations, enforce our terms, and protect the rights and safety of SBFA, our members, and others.

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as described here:

  • Service providers. We use third parties to run the Site and our programs, including hosting (Cloudflare), forms (Tally and FormSubmit), membership management (Memberstack), payments (Stripe), and email. They may process information on our behalf and are expected to use it only to provide their services to us.
  • Partners. Where a partner needs to confirm that you are an SBFA member to honor an offer, we may confirm your membership status. We do not give partners your contact details for their own marketing without your permission.
  • Chapter leaders. If you join through a chapter leader's referral, we may tell that leader that your signup occurred so the referral can be credited.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law or legal process, or to protect rights, property, or safety.
  • Business changes. If SBFA reorganizes, merges, or transfers its operations, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

4. Third-Party Services and Links

Forms and tools on the Site are provided by third parties, and those providers handle the information you submit under their own privacy policies. The Site also links to partner and affiliate websites that we do not control. Please review their privacy practices before sharing information with them.

5. Cookies, Pixels, and Affiliate Tracking

Some links and offers on the Site are affiliate links. When you click one, the partner or its affiliate network (for example, CJ Affiliate) may place cookies or use tracking pixels to record that your visit came from SBFA, so that a commission can be credited to us. These technologies may collect information such as your IP address, browser details, and the pages you view. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, and many browsers offer a "Do Not Track" or similar signal, though we do not currently respond to those signals. Blocking cookies may affect how some Site features work.

6. Data Retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide the Services, maintain membership and payment records, resolve disputes, and meet legal, tax, and accounting requirements. When information is no longer needed, we delete or de-identify it.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures, and rely on reputable providers, to help protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send sensitive information such as Social Security numbers or full payment card numbers through general contact or referral forms.

8. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access and updates. You may ask us to access, correct, or update the personal information we hold about you.
  • Deletion. You may ask us to delete your personal information, subject to information we must keep for legal, tax, or legitimate business reasons.
  • Emails. You can opt out of non-essential emails by following the unsubscribe instructions in the message. We may still send service-related messages, such as receipts and renewal notices.
  • State privacy rights. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state privacy laws. To exercise any right, contact us using the information below, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

The Site and Services are intended for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page with a new effective date, and may notify members of material changes by email or on the Site.
