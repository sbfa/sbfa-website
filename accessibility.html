Accessibility Statement

Effective date: September 23, 2026

The Small Business Fleet Alliance is committed to making our website usable for everyone, including people with disabilities. We want every member and visitor to be able to find information, review offers, and join without unnecessary barriers.

Our Approach

We are working toward meeting the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. Our site is built with plain, semantic HTML and CSS, and we aim to provide readable text and color contrast, a logical heading structure, descriptive link text, keyboard-navigable pages, and layouts that adapt to different screen sizes and zoom levels.

Known Limitations

We are a small organization and our site is still developing, so some pages or features may not yet be fully accessible. In particular, forms and tools provided by third parties (such as our sign-up and referral forms and payment pages) are controlled by those providers, and we cannot guarantee their accessibility, although we choose providers with accessibility in mind where we can.

Feedback and Assistance

If you have trouble using any part of the Site, or need information in a different format, please tell us. Include the page address and a description of the problem, and we will do our best to respond promptly and provide the information or help you need through another route, such as by email or phone.

Email: gwillis@sbfaohio.com

We review this statement and our pages periodically and will update it as we make improvements.
