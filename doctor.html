24/7 Virtual Telehealth Consults

FLAT $29–$45 VISITS • NO INSURANCE NEEDED

1. 24/7 Telehealth & Same-Day Prescription Routing

Skip expensive ER copays and lost route time. Connect with licensed medical doctors via video or phone from your driver seat for urgent sick visits, rashes, minor injuries, and fast prescription refills.

  • ✓ Upfront Transparent Cash Rates: Pay a simple flat fee ($25 to $45) per visit with any debit or credit card—no hidden facility fees.
  • ✓ Zero Monthly Subscription Friction: Book on demand when you need care without being locked into expensive monthly retainers.
  • ✓ Route-Friendly Rx: Prescriptions can be sent directly to any pharmacy along your delivery route.

How to Access Virtual Care on Route:

  1. Select an on-demand telehealth provider (Sesame Care or HealthTap).
  2. Choose your appointment time (immediate 15-minute slots or scheduled) and state location.
  3. Enter the address of the nearest pharmacy along your route for prescription dispatch.

Direct Primary Care (DPC)

MEMBERSHIP MEDICINE • $0 COPAYS

2. Direct Primary Care Local Family Doctors

Direct Primary Care bypasses health insurance completely. You pay a low monthly membership fee ($60–$90/month) directly to a local clinic for unlimited doctor visits, wholesale lab tests, and direct texting/calling with your personal physician.

  • ✓ Unlimited Office Visits: Schedule in-person checkups, physicals, and chronic disease management with $0 per-visit copays.
  • ✓ Direct Physician Phone Access: Call or text your doctor when health questions arise while you are out on the road.
  • ✓ Wholesale Prescription & Lab Pricing: Access routine bloodwork and common generics at up to 90% below commercial rates.

Find a DPC Doctor Near Your Terminal:

  1. Enter your home terminal or city zip code to locate independent DPC practices nearby.
  2. Contact the clinic to enroll in a direct doctor-patient membership.

Catastrophic HealthShare Options

AFFORDABLE MAJOR MEDICAL ALTERNATIVE

4. HealthShare Communities (ER & Surgery)

Traditional individual ACA insurance can cost $600–$1,200/month with large deductibles. Modern non-insurance health-sharing networks pool member funds to share major hospital bills, ER visits, and surgeries for $150–$300/month.

  • ✓ Zion HealthShare: Modern, non-religious health-sharing community with straightforward guidelines for major medical and hospitalization costs.
  • ✓ CrowdHealth: Tech-forward crowdfunding alternative designed for self-employed entrepreneurs and 1099 contractors.
  • ✓ Year-Round Enrollment: Sign up any day of the year without waiting for open enrollment deadlines.
