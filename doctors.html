1. 24/7 Telehealth & Same-Day Prescription Routing

How it saves time & money: Skip expensive emergency room copays and lost route time. Connect with licensed medical doctors via video or phone from your driver seat for urgent sick visits, rashes, minor injuries, and fast prescription refills.

  • ✓ Same-Day Pharmacy Routing: Doctors call in antibiotics, blood pressure maintenance meds, and acute treatments to any roadside pharmacy or truck stop within minutes.
  • ✓ Upfront Transparent Cash Rates: Pay a simple flat fee ($25 to $45) per visit directly with any debit or credit card—no hidden facility fees or surprise bills.
  • ✓ Zero Monthly Subscription Friction: Book on demand when you need care without being locked into expensive monthly retainer contracts.

How to Access Virtual Care on Route:

  1. Select an on-demand telehealth provider (Sesame Care or HealthTap).
  2. Choose your appointment time (immediate 15-minute queue or scheduled slot) and state location.
  3. Enter the address of the nearest pharmacy along your delivery route for prescription dispatch.

MEMBERSHIP MEDICINE • $0 COPAYS

2. Direct Primary Care (DPC) Local Family Doctors

How it works: Direct Primary Care bypasses health insurance completely. You pay a low monthly membership fee ($60–$90/month) directly to a local clinic for unlimited doctor visits, wholesale lab tests, and direct texting/calling with your personal physician.

  • ✓ Unlimited Office Visits: Schedule in-person checkups, physicals, and chronic disease management visits with $0 per-visit copays.
  • ✓ Direct Physician Phone Access: Call or text your doctor directly when health questions arise while you are out on the road.
  • ✓ Wholesale Prescription & Lab Pricing: Access routine bloodwork, panels, and common generic prescriptions at up to 90% below commercial lab rates.

Find a DPC Doctor in Your Area:

  1. Open the free DPC Frontier Mapper database.
  2. Enter your home terminal or city zip code to locate independent DPC practices nearby.
  3. Contact the clinic to enroll in a direct doctor-patient membership.

4. Catastrophic HealthShare Communities (ER & Surgery)

How it protects your revenue: Traditional individual ACA insurance can cost $600 to $1,200/month with huge deductibles. Modern non-insurance health-sharing networks pool member funds to share major hospital bills, emergency room visits, and surgeries for $150 to $300/month.

  • ✓ Zion HealthShare: Modern, non-religious health-sharing community with straightforward guidelines for sharing major medical and hospitalization costs.
  • ✓ CrowdHealth: Tech-forward crowdfunding alternative designed specifically for self-employed entrepreneurs and 1099 contractors to negotiate and crowdfund medical events.
  • ✓ Year-Round Enrollment: Sign up any day of the year without waiting for open enrollment deadlines.
