Chapter Leader Rewards

Direct income from day one — plus milestone bonuses as your chapter grows.

1. Local Territory Direct Cuts

Earned directly on every driver in your local chapter territory

  • Commercial Pro — Standard Annual ($200/yr)
    $100 upfront + $100 on each annual renewal
    Box trucks, cargo vans, Sprinters, semis · 2.0 credits
  • Commercial Pro — Founder Special ($150/yr)
    $75 upfront + $75 on each annual renewal
    Limited Founding member rate (50/50 revenue split) · 2.0 credits
  • Commercial Pro — Monthly ($20/mo)
    $7.00/month while they stay active
    Box trucks, cargo vans, Sprinters, semis · 1.0 credit
  • Gig Tier — Annual ($60/yr)
    $30 upfront + $30 on each annual renewal
    Sedans, SUVs, minivans · DoorDash, Spark, Flex, etc. · 1.0 credit
  • Gig Tier — Monthly ($7/mo)
    $2.50/month while they stay active
    Sedans, SUVs, minivans · DoorDash, Spark, Flex, etc. · 0.5 credit

2. Founding 100 National Residuals

Earned by Founding Members when referring drivers outside their local territory

  • National Commercial Pro Referral
    $50.00 / year recurring residual
    Earned when referring Pro drivers in unassigned or external territories
  • National Gig Tier Referral
    $20.00 / year recurring residual
    Earned when referring Gig drivers in unassigned or external territories

*Strict single-tier policy: Local territory signups pay Chapter Leader cuts; out-of-territory signups pay Founding residuals. Payouts do not double-dip.

3. Chapter Reserve & Milestone Cash Vault

Credits stack toward milestone cash bonuses as your chapter grows.

15 Credits → $250 Chapter Launch Bonus

One-time cash bonus awarded to kickstart your territory when initial chapter milestones are unlocked (Fast-track: 8 annual Commercial Pro members).

25 Credits → $100 Service Partner Bounties

+$100 cash per local mechanic, tire shop, or towing company you onboard (capped at 3 partners / $300 max).

50 Credits → Fuel Gallonage Overrides

Unlocks ongoing quarterly cash overrides on gallons pumped by chapter members through network fuel partners like Mudflap.

Local Events → 100% Sponsor Cash

Host driver meetups, safety talks, or breakfasts — keep 100% of vendor sponsorship fees.

4. Payout Schedule

  • Direct baseline income & residuals — Paid monthly after a 30-day clearing window
  • Milestone bonuses & fuel overrides — Paid quarterly (Jan 15, Apr 15, Jul 15, Oct 15)
