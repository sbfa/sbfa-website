What a Chapter Leader Does

Simple role. Real independence. Clear framework.

1. Recruit Members

Bring independent drivers and operators in your area into the Alliance. That’s the core of the role.

2. Set Up Local Partners

Connect with local shops, tire places, and service providers so members can get better local pricing.

3. Run It Your Way

You’re independent. Run the chapter the way that works for your city — as long as you stay inside the SBFA framework.

4. Stick to the Framework

Use the brand, pricing structure, residual rules, and basic standards. Everything else is yours to build.

What You Get

Territory Income

  • Direct cuts on members in your local territory
  • Recurring income while they stay active
  • Milestone bonuses as the chapter grows

Support & Tools

  • Brand and positioning
  • Member benefits and deals to offer
  • Billing and residual tracking handled centrally

How the Money Works

Direct income from members in your territory, plus bonuses as you grow.
